Research Highlights

Quantum Defects in 2D Materials

Investigating the broadband relaxation dynamics of Boron-Vacancy centers in hexagonal Boron Nitride. I use ab-initio spin-phonon relaxation theory to compute coupling coefficients and phonon-limited relaxation times.

Unconventional Superconductivity

Predicting gap symmetries (e.g., d-wave, f-wave) in cuprates and nickelates. My recent work explores unique dxy-wave superconductivity in Ba2CuO3.25 and orbital-selective pairing mechanisms.

Research Areas & Methods

  • I study unconventional superconductivity in cuprates and infinite-layer nickelates, using spin-fluctuation mechanisms to predict gap symmetries (e.g., d-wave) and to assess rare-earth f-orbital effects on the superconducting state.
  • I investigate spin–phonon physics and quantum defects—especially the VB- center in h-BN—by computing DFT-based coupling spectra and phonon-limited T1 relaxation.
  • My workflow integrates ab-initio and many-body methods: DFT/DFPT, Wannierization, multiband Hubbard, RPA, BdG, and Eliashberg theory to probe emergent phases in correlated materials.
  • I also explore interlayer excitons in 2D heterostructures and moiré transport phenomena such as weak localization in graphene/h-BN bilayers.
  • Methodologically, I build scalable, reproducible HPC pipelines in Python/Fortran around VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO, Phonopy/EPW, and Wannier90, parallelized with MPI/OpenMP/SLURM.
